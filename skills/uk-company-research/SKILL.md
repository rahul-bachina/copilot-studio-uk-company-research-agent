# UK Company Research

Confirm the company and listing, then apply the `uk-equity-evidence` discipline.

Produce a report containing identity and scope, an executive research summary, business model and segments, geographic exposure, strategy and competition, management and governance, dated financial and valuation evidence, recent developments, catalysts, risks, unresolved questions, and a source register.

Use a 12-month horizon unless the user specifies another. Separate reported facts from inference. Do not assign Positive, Neutral, Negative, Buy, Hold, or Sell labels. Do not fill gaps with estimates.
